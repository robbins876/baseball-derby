Read the game rules

Each turn has 7 hittable pitches. Replace unhittable pitches without recording them. Tap Hit for a ball hit in the air beyond the infield (+1 point); otherwise tap No hit. The current ball has a yellow halo, hits turn blue, and no hits turn orange. After pitch 7, choose Home Run Bonus (+2 to the current batter) or No bonus. Players bat in order. Use Undo to correct a tap.
